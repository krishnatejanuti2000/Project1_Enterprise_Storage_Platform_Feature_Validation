RAID 10 LAB — MOUNT RAID 10 FILESYSTEM
======================================

MOUNT POINT
-----------
/mnt/raid10


COMMANDS
--------
sudo mkdir -p /mnt/raid10

sudo mount /dev/md10 /mnt/raid10


RESULT
------
Mount completed successfully.

    /dev/md10

Filesystem:
    ext4

Mount point:
    /mnt/raid10

Access mode:
    read/write
