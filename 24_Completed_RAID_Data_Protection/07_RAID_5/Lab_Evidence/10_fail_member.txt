============================================================
RAID 5 LAB — SIMULATE MEMBER FAILURE
============================================================

Purpose:
Intentionally mark /dev/sdc as a failed RAID 5 member in
order to validate degraded-mode operation and RAID 5
single-member fault tolerance.

Target RAID Array:

    /dev/md0

Target Member:

    /dev/sdc


============================================================
COMMAND
============================================================

sudo mdadm --manage /dev/md0 --fail /dev/sdc


============================================================
COMMAND OUTPUT
============================================================

No output was produced by the command.

The command completed successfully.


============================================================
FAILURE SIMULATION
============================================================

The RAID management command instructed mdadm to mark:

    /dev/sdc

as failed within:

    /dev/md0


============================================================
NEXT VALIDATION
============================================================

The RAID state was subsequently checked using:

    cat /proc/mdstat

The detailed degraded state was also checked using:

    sudo mdadm --detail /dev/md0


============================================================
RESULT
============================================================

The intentional RAID member failure operation completed
successfully.

The subsequent RAID status verification confirmed that
/dev/sdc entered the failed state and the RAID 5 array
continued operating in degraded mode.


============================================================
IMPORTANT NOTE
============================================================

This was a software-level failure simulation performed with
mdadm.

The physical disk was not physically damaged.


============================================================
END OF EVIDENCE
============================================================
