============================================================
RAID 1 Validation Lab
Step 12 - Verify Degraded Mode
============================================================

Commands:
---------

cat /proc/mdstat

sudo mdadm --detail /dev/md1

Purpose:
--------

Verify that the RAID array remains operational after a member disk failure.

Observation:
------------

- RAID status changed to degraded.
- Active Devices: 1
- Working Devices: 1
- Failed Devices: 1
- Data remained available through the surviving mirror member.

Result:
-------

PASS

RAID 1 fault tolerance verified successfully.
