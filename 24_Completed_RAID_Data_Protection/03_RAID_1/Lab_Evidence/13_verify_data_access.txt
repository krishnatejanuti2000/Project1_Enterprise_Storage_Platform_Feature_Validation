============================================================
RAID 1 Validation Lab
Step 13 - Verify Data Accessibility During Disk Failure
============================================================

Commands:
---------

ls -lh /mnt/raid1

head -c 64 /mnt/raid1/testfile.img | xxd

Purpose:
--------

Verify that application data remains accessible after one RAID member has failed.

Observation:
------------

- The test file remained present in the filesystem.
- The file was successfully read.
- Binary output confirmed that data was accessible through the surviving mirror member.
- RAID 1 continued serving application I/O despite operating in degraded mode.

Result:
-------

PASS

Data accessibility successfully verified during degraded mode.
