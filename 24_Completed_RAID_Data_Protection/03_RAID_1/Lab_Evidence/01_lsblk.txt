============================================================
RAID 1 Validation Lab
Step 01 - Verify Available Block Devices
============================================================

Command:
--------

lsblk

Purpose:
--------

Verify that the required physical disks are available before creating the RAID 1 array.

Observation:
------------

- /dev/sdb is available.
- /dev/sde is available.
- Existing RAID 0 array (md0) is present on /dev/sdc and /dev/sdd.
- No active RAID 1 array exists.
- Both selected disks are ready for RAID 1 creation after cleanup.

Result:
-------

PASS

The required disks were successfully identified for RAID 1 validation.
