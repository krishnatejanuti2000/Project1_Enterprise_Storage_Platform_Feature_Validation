============================================================
RAID 1 Validation Lab
Step 09 - Mount RAID Filesystem
============================================================

Commands:
---------

sudo mkdir -p /mnt/raid1

sudo mount /dev/md1 /mnt/raid1

df -h

Purpose:
--------

Mount the RAID 1 filesystem and verify that it is accessible through the operating system.

Observation:
------------

- Mount point (/mnt/raid1) was created.
- RAID filesystem mounted successfully.
- The mounted filesystem appeared in the output of df -h.
- The logical RAID device is ready for I/O operations.

Result:
-------

PASS

RAID 1 filesystem mounted successfully.
