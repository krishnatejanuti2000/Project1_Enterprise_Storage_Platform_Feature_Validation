============================================================
RAID 1 Validation Lab
Step 04 - Verify Clean Member Disks
============================================================

Commands:
---------

lsblk

sudo mdadm --examine /dev/sdb

sudo mdadm --examine /dev/sde

Purpose:
--------

Verify that both member disks contain no RAID metadata before creating the RAID 1 array.

Observation:
------------

- Both disks are visible as standalone block devices.
- No RAID superblocks were detected.
- Disks are ready for RAID creation.

Result:
-------

PASS

Both disks successfully verified as clean RAID members.
