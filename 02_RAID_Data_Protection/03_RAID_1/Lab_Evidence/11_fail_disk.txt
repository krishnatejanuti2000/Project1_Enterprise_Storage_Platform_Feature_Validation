============================================================
RAID 1 Validation Lab
Step 11 - Simulate Disk Failure
============================================================

Commands:
---------

sudo mdadm --manage /dev/md1 --fail /dev/sde

cat /proc/mdstat

sudo mdadm --detail /dev/md1

Purpose:
--------

Simulate the failure of one RAID member to validate RAID 1 fault tolerance.

Observation:
------------

- /dev/sde was successfully marked as failed.
- RAID entered degraded mode.
- Health indicator changed from [UU] to [U_].
- One member disk continued serving application I/O.

Result:
-------

PASS

Disk failure simulation completed successfully.
