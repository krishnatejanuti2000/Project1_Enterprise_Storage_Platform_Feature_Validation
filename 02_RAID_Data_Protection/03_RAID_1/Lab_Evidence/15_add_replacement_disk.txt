============================================================
RAID 1 Validation Lab
Step 15 - Add Replacement Disk
============================================================

Commands:
---------

sudo mdadm --manage /dev/md1 --add /dev/sde

Purpose:
--------

Add the replacement disk to the RAID array and initiate recovery.

Observation:
------------

- /dev/sde was successfully added to the RAID array.
- Automatic recovery (rebuild) started immediately.
- The RAID controller began copying data from the healthy mirror member to the replacement disk.

Result:
-------

PASS

Replacement disk successfully added and recovery initiated.
