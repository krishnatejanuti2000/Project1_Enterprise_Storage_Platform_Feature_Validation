============================================================
RAID 1 Validation Lab
Step 03 - Remove Inactive RAID Array
============================================================

Command:
--------

sudo mdadm --stop /dev/md127

Purpose:
--------

Stop the automatically assembled inactive RAID device to avoid conflicts during RAID 1 creation.

Observation:
------------

- md127 was successfully stopped.
- The inactive RAID device no longer exists.

Result:
-------

PASS

Inactive RAID array removed successfully.
