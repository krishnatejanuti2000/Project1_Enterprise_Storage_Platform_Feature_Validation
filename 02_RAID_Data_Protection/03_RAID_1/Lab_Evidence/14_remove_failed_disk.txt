============================================================
RAID 1 Validation Lab
Step 14 - Remove Failed RAID Member
============================================================

Commands:
---------

sudo mdadm --manage /dev/md1 --remove /dev/sde

cat /proc/mdstat

sudo mdadm --detail /dev/md1

Purpose:
--------

Remove the failed RAID member from the array before adding a replacement disk.

Observation:
------------

- The failed member (/dev/sde) was successfully removed.
- RAID continued operating with a single healthy mirror member.
- The array remained in degraded mode while waiting for a replacement disk.

Result:
-------

PASS

Failed RAID member removed successfully.
