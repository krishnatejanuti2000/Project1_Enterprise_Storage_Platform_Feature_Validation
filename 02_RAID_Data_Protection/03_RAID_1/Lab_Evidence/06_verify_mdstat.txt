============================================================
RAID 1 Validation Lab
Step 06 - Verify RAID Status
============================================================

Command:
--------

cat /proc/mdstat

Purpose:
--------

Verify that the RAID 1 array is active and monitor the initial synchronization process.

Observation:
------------

- RAID 1 array (md1) is active.
- Both member disks are present.
- Health status is [UU].
- Initial resynchronization is in progress.

Result:
-------

PASS

RAID 1 array is operational and synchronization started successfully.
