RAID 6 LAB — ENVIRONMENT ANOMALY
================================

INCIDENT
--------
During the first RAID 6 creation/resynchronization attempt, an
external SSH framework/cleanup process interfered with the lab disks.

OBSERVED SYMPTOMS
-----------------
The RAID 6 array was initially created successfully.

During the later lab session:

    /proc/mdstat
    ↓
    RAID array disappeared

Subsequent checks showed:

    mdadm --examine /dev/sdb /dev/sdc /dev/sdd /dev/sde
    ↓
    No md superblock detected

CAUSE
-----
The external SSH framework/cleanup process had removed the existing
RAID metadata from the lab disks.

ADDITIONAL OBSERVATION
----------------------
/dev/sdb was also found mounted as:

    /mnt/sdb

This caused an initial RAID creation attempt to fail with:

    Device or resource busy

The filesystem was unmounted before the successful RAID 6 recreation.

RESOLUTION
----------
1. Confirmed the RAID lab disks were safe to reuse.
2. Unmounted /dev/sdb from /mnt/sdb.
3. Removed the remaining ext4 signature from /dev/sdb.
4. Verified all four lab disks were clean.
5. Recreated the RAID 6 array as /dev/md6.
6. Completed initial synchronization.
7. Created the ext4 filesystem.
8. Mounted /dev/md6 at /mnt/raid6.
9. Completed the full RAID 6 failure/rebuild validation.

IMPACT
------
No RAID 6 test data from the successful validation run was lost.

The anomaly occurred before the successful test-data and failure-
recovery validation cycle.

FINAL STATUS
------------
Resolved.

The successful RAID 6 lab completed with:

    4/4 active members
    0 failed members
    [UUUU]
    Data integrity PASS
