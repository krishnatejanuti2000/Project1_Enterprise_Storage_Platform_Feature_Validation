============================================================
RAID 5 LAB — REMOVE FAILED MEMBER
============================================================

Purpose:
Remove the failed RAID member /dev/sdc from the degraded
RAID 5 array after verifying its failed state.

RAID Array:

    /dev/md0

Failed Member:

    /dev/sdc


============================================================
COMMAND
============================================================

sudo mdadm --manage /dev/md0 --remove /dev/sdc


============================================================
OUTPUT
============================================================

mdadm: hot removed /dev/sdc from /dev/md0


============================================================
VERIFICATION
============================================================

The mdadm command successfully removed /dev/sdc from the
active RAID configuration.

At this point:

    /dev/sdb → active
    /dev/sdc → removed
    /dev/sdd → active
    /dev/sde → active


The RAID array remained degraded because the replacement
member had not yet been added.


============================================================
NEXT STEP
============================================================

The RAID state was subsequently verified using:

    cat /proc/mdstat

The resulting state showed:

    [4/3] [U_UU]


============================================================
RESULT
============================================================

The failed member /dev/sdc was successfully hot-removed from
the RAID 5 array.

The array remained operational in degraded mode with three
active members.


============================================================
END OF EVIDENCE
============================================================
