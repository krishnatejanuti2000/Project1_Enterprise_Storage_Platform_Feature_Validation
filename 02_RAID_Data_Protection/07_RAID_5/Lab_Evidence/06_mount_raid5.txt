============================================================
RAID 5 LAB — MOUNT RAID 5 FILESYSTEM
============================================================

Purpose:
Mount the ext4 filesystem created on /dev/md0 at the
dedicated RAID 5 mount point.

Command:

sudo mkdir -p /mnt/raid5

Output:

No output.


Command:

sudo mount /dev/md0 /mnt/raid5

Output:

No output.


============================================================
VERIFICATION
============================================================

Mount Point:

    /mnt/raid5

RAID Device:

    /dev/md0

Filesystem:

    ext4


============================================================
RESULT
============================================================

The mount-point directory was created successfully.

The ext4 filesystem on /dev/md0 was successfully mounted at:

    /mnt/raid5

A successful mount command produced no error output.
