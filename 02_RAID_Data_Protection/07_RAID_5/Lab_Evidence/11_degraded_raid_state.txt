============================================================
RAID 5 LAB — DEGRADED RAID STATE
============================================================

Purpose:
Verify that RAID 5 correctly enters degraded mode after one
member is intentionally failed.

Command:

cat /proc/mdstat


============================================================
OUTPUT
============================================================

Personalities : [raid0] [raid1] [raid4] [raid5] [raid6] [raid10] [linear]

md0 : active raid5 sde[4] sdd[2] sdc[1](F) sdb[0]
      527081472 blocks super 1.2 level 5, 512k chunk, algorithm 2 [4/3] [U_UU]
      bitmap: 1/2 pages [4KB], 65536KB chunk

unused devices: <none>


============================================================
VERIFICATION
============================================================

RAID Level:

    RAID 5


Configured RAID Devices:

    4


Currently Active/Usable Members:

    3


Failed Member:

    /dev/sdc


Healthy Members:

    /dev/sdb
    /dev/sdd
    /dev/sde


RAID State:

    Degraded


RAID Status:

    [4/3]


Member Bitmap:

    [U_UU]


============================================================
MEMBER INTERPRETATION
============================================================

/dev/sdb → U → healthy
/dev/sdc → F → failed
/dev/sdd → U → healthy
/dev/sde → U → healthy


The "(F)" marker identifies /dev/sdc as the failed member.

The [U_UU] state indicates that one RAID member position is
unavailable while the remaining three members are operational.


============================================================
RESULT
============================================================

RAID 5 successfully entered degraded mode after the failure
of one member.

The array remained operational with three working members,
demonstrating RAID 5 single-member failure tolerance.
